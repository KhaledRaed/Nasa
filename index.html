About Us

We are a team of Data Analysts, AI enthusiasts, and Web Developers from the University of Jordan, brought together by our interest in discovery and technology. Our goal is to use machine learning and AI to make sense of large astronomical datasets. With skills in data analysis, AI model building, and web development, we work together to solve the challenge of exoplanet identification.
