Comparative Exoplanet Analysis

Habitable Zone Candidates

Planets like TRAPPIST-1 e and Kepler-186 f show low Mass/Radius ratios and moderate temperatures, highlighting them as prime candidates for follow-up habitability studies.

The Ultra-Hots

WASP-12 b's profile reveals an extreme case: high mass, high radius, and a dangerously short orbital period, leading to its intense, tidally-locked temperature profile.

Parameter Correlation

A noticeable inverse relationship exists between Orbital Period and Temperature, a classic finding for exoplanets: closer orbits lead to extremely high surface temperatures.
